EU website has images of coin and banknote faces, but the banknotes have SPECIMEN written on top of them.
This PDF has the sides without watermark at a very low resolution:
https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/other/euroleafleten.pdf
we import it at 32dpi and extract the banknotes.

Coins source: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/coins/common/html/index.en.html
we download the common variant (not new-common) as our extracted banknotes are also the old version.
Background is removed (tolerance: 30%) and image is rescaled to 6 % of the original size.
Alpha is removed using Assets/Textures/Teachers/removeAlpha.py and background is switched to white.
